# Team Profiles instead of founder names

The team is the strongest Factor at pre-seed. But founders have not agreed to appear in a private database, and the GDPR requires data minimisation and an information duty toward the people concerned (Art. 14). The radar therefore stores anonymous Team Profiles. Names exist only as Contacts, once the Owner is personally in touch. When the Owner Passes on a startup, the radar deletes its Contact data automatically after 12 months; the startup's other data stays.
